why is this website so simple?
- i made this for the sake of having a place to put words down. there's no need to learn any web frameworks or complicate things when i just want to have a place to vent on the internet
- some modern websites sort of lack personality and focus too hard on "minimalism"
- i liked browsing simple static websites from neocities, professors from my university, or old music critics like scaruffi and christgau
- nowadays my phone is in grayscale and i have eink devices, so having a website that looks similar on those devices is pretty cool - im not really good at expressing myself anyways

why did i make this website?
- its nice to have a place to put thoughts. i do have a youtube channel for long-form ranty type of stuff about anything but text can be a bit more random and easier to consume for some people
- i've always hated writing so this is some sort of practice for me to learn how to better convert thoughts into words
- i usually keep to myself so this is an outlet for me to yap without considering what another human being is thinking. people can choose what to read and stop anytime they aren't interested. people reading actually want that information so i dont have to have any confidence or self doubt or something
- im not really an artistic guy so this is an outlet somewhat to fill that void since it is desireable to be a creator not a consumer
